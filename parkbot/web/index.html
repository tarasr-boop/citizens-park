How this works

The oval at {{park_short}} is a dog off-lead area, but dogs must stay off it while an organised activity is on. They are never allowed in the playground.

Booked times come from the council's {{park_short}} page. The council says the oval is open to the public outside those hours, and the lights stay on until {{lights_off}}.

This page reads the council's page several times a day, from early morning to the evening. "Last checked" at the top is the last time that worked.

The council lists one month at a time, and a new month can appear late. On a day its page does not cover yet, this page shows the usual weekly pattern instead and marks it usual. Treat those times as likely, not certain.
